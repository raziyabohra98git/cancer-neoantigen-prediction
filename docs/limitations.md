# Scientific and Technical Limitations

## 1. Synthetic demonstration data

All records generated by the public demo are synthetic. The results are
intended to demonstrate pipeline execution and software quality control,
not to report findings from real patients.

## 2. Binding prediction is not immunogenicity

Predicted MHC class I binding does not establish that a peptide is processed,
presented on a tumor cell, recognized by T cells, or capable of producing an
immune response. Experimental validation is outside this demonstration.

## 3. Priority categories are rule-based

Priority labels depend on predefined binding and mutant-versus-WT thresholds.
They are computational filtering categories, not clinical classifications.

## 4. AI ranking limitations

The demonstration trains and scores its models on the same synthetic dataset.
The priority labels are derived from rules related to the model features.
Consequently, the resulting scores are not an independent assessment of
immunogenicity and do not measure prospective generalization.

A rigorous predictive evaluation would require an appropriate independent
evaluation design, leakage controls, suitable ground-truth labels, and
clearly defined performance metrics.

## 5. HLA and biological context

HLA typing accuracy, tumor expression, antigen processing, peptide
presentation, and patient-specific immune context can affect real candidate
assessment. These biological factors cannot be established solely by this
synthetic demonstration.

## 6. Pathway analysis

Pathway enrichment is skipped for placeholder gene symbols in the public
demo. Synthetic output must not be used to claim biological pathway
enrichment or disease mechanisms.

## 7. Data governance

Real patient-level results, controlled-access data, and internship-derived
outputs must not be published without confirming applicable access terms,
data permissions, and authorization from the relevant project owner.

## 8. Intended use

This repository is an educational and portfolio demonstration of data
handling, quality control, rule-based prioritization, and AI-assisted
ranking. It is not a clinical decision-support tool and must not be used
to make patient treatment decisions.
